MODEL ORGANISMS
01
FOCAL EXPERIMENT

Zhang et al. · Nature Communications · 2021

Adult male mice exposed to a 0.29 ± 0.01 µT hypomagnetic field versus a 55.26 ± 0.05 µT local control field. The researchers reported reduced adult hippocampal neurogenesis and selected cognitive impairments, with some reversibility.

READ PEER-REVIEWED PAPER ↗
01 /OBSERVED IN MICE

Adult neurogenesis

Reduced proliferation of certain adult neural stem cells was reported under long-term experimental weak-field exposure.

NOT A HUMAN EFFECT ESTIMATE
02 /CELL-SPECIFIC FINDING

ROS signaling

The 2021 paper reported lower reactive oxygen species within studied neural stem cells. ROS behavior differs by cell type and context.

DIRECTION MUST BE SCOPED
03 /PROPOSED MECHANISM

Mitochondrial pathways

A 2026 review discusses mitochondria and iron-sulfur clusters as candidate pathways, not a confirmed universal mechanism.

HYPOTHESIS, NOT CAUSAL PROOF
04 /UNKNOWN HUMAN RELEVANCE

Today's terrestrial field

No demonstrated causal link from today's field weakening to declining human cognition. Mouse exposure is not a human safety threshold.

DO NOT INFER HEALTH RISK
≠

Similar words are not equivalent measurements.

Hypomagnetic mouse laboratory conditions, Swarm geomagnetic models, ground sensor readings, Kp indices and neural electrical activity describe different things. GBRM keeps their measurement types and provenance separate.
